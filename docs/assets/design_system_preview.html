04

Rules

A small set of enforceable rules keeps the system coherent after the first week. Each one has a visible failure mode.

DODepth from surface + hairline

Use base, raised, inset and overlay. One 1px border marks the edge. Menus and floating nodes are the only shadows.

DON'TStack elevation or radii

No 12px radius on a dock, no three shadows on a card, no gradient border. If two surfaces are unclear, add one surface step.

DOKeep the 36 / 30 / 24 rhythm

Strip 36, control 30, chip 24, icon 12, row 28. The rhythm is what makes every dock feel related.

DON'TLet a form grow controls

44px inputs and 16px gaps turn an instrument into a web form. Remove a control or split the panel; do not inflate it.

DOReserve accent for state

Selection, focus, checked pin, active tab, active path. One accent per view is the target.

DON'TUse accent to decorate

Category identity belongs to the node palette. Headers, icons and chips stay neutral unless they are interactive or active.

DOStatus only where status lives

A dot, a badge, a ring, a single log token. Success and error do not repaint a whole dock.

DON'TEncode state in colour alone

Pair the ring with a dot or label. Pair a failed node with a glyph. Pair a log level with its word.

Content and interaction
Live by default. Check, move and select apply immediately; text search debounces at 300ms. History records only a real diff and reads as Noun Verbed (“Column Selection Changed”). An Apply button is a missing signal, not a safety feature.
  • Sentence case for labels and tooltips; Title Case for window and dialog titles.
  • Menu items that open a dialog end in an ellipsis. Commands that act immediately do not.
  • Counts read 1,284 rows · 12 cols: thousands separator, thin middot, unit after the number.
  • Empty states are one sentence plus, at most, one hint line. No illustration.
  • Tooltips are sentence case and take no trailing period; accessibleName matches the tooltip.
  • Never use QIcon.fromTheme on Windows. Bundle the glyph or use the :/qss_icons fallback.
  • Focus is visible: 1px accent border plus a 2px soft ring. Keyboard shortcuts never replace focus.
