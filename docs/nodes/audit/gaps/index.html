Gaps¶

Purpose¶

Finds what is missing from a sequence, such as cheque or journal numbers, or days without entries.

Inputs and outputs¶

One input. Outputs G Gaps (the findings) and S Summary.

Settings¶

Section Label Default Effect
Sequence Column none The column holding the numbers (or, for Date, the dates). Required.
Sequence Type Prefixed Number: whole numbers (1042, 1042.0). Prefixed: text then digits, such as JE-000123; the series is the prefix. Date: calendar days with no entry.
Group by column list with All / None none Check each series separately, for example one per ledger or branch.
Also report (not Date) Repeated numbers on Report a number that appears more than once.
Also report (not Date) Numbers out of order on Report a number lower than a higher one seen earlier in the same series. This depends on the order the rows arrive in.
Expected range (not Date) First / Last blank ("Lowest seen" / "Highest seen") The first and last number you expect, so a gap at the very start or end is reported too. Last may not be below First (TD-AUDIT-004).
Dates (Date only) Format %Y-%m-%d How text dates are written.
Dates Smallest gap 1 day Ignore gaps shorter than this many days (1 to 3660).
Dates Skip weekends off Saturdays and Sundays are not expected, so they do not count as missing.
Dates Holidays empty One date per line, YYYY-MM-DD; shown only when Skip weekends is on. A bad line stops the node (TD-AUDIT-004).

Prefixed numbers keep their zero padding: JE-0003 is reported, not JE-3, as long as the series has zero-padded numbers. A series written 1, 2, ... 12 stays unpadded. Cells that are empty are ignored; text that does not fit the pattern is counted as unreadable (TD-AUDIT-102 warning).

Outputs.

  • G Gaps: one row per finding, never one row per missing number. Columns: the Group by columns, then prefix, finding, from, to, count, source_row. finding is gap (a missing range from from to to, count numbers), duplicate (count times; source_row is the 1-based input row of the first occurrence), out_of_order (source_row is the offending row) or date_gap (days with no entry). source_row is blank for gaps. Rows are sorted by group, prefix, finding and then by the numeric value of from, so 6..7 comes before 11..12 (dates sort chronologically).
  • S Summary: the eleven standard columns (population_value and exception_value are blank; an "exception" is one finding row) plus series, gap_ranges, missing_items, duplicate_values, out_of_order_rows, unparseable_rows. Status is fail when there is any finding.

Worked example¶

Type Prefixed, column je, Group by ledger. Input:

ledger je
GL JE-0001
GL JE-0002
GL JE-0005
GL JE-0006
GL JE-0006
GL JE-0004
AP JE-0100
AP JE-0102

G returns:

ledger prefix finding from to count source_row
AP JE- gap JE-0101 JE-0101 1
GL JE- duplicate JE-0006 JE-0006 2 4
GL JE- gap JE-0003 JE-0003 1
GL JE- out_of_order JE-0004 JE-0004 1 6

S: fail, "2 gap ranges covering 2 missing numbers; 1 repeated numbers; 1 numbers out of order." (series 2, exception_rows 4 of 8 rows).

Expected range: for the numbers 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10 with type Number, First 1 and Last 12, G reports the gaps 1..2, 6..7 and 11..12.

Date example: type Date, column posting with 2025-03-03, 03-04, 03-07, 03-12 and 03-12 again: G has two date_gap rows, 2025-03-05..2025-03-06 (2 days) and 2025-03-08..2025-03-11 (4 days). Switching on Skip weekends with one holiday, 2025-03-10, leaves 2025-03-05..2025-03-06 (2) and 2025-03-11..2025-03-11 (1): the weekend and the holiday are not missing days.
